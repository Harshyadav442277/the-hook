# 60–90 second demo script

## 0–10 seconds — Problem

“It is the 12th inning of the World Series, the bases are loaded, and one pitching decision can decide the game. THE HOOK answers one question: who should the manager use right now?”

## 10–25 seconds — Situation

“The Dodgers and Blue Jays are tied 5–5 with two outs. Emmet Sheehan is on the mound; Nathan Lukes, Vladimir Guerrero Jr., and Isiah Kiner-Falefa are next. The manager chose Clayton Kershaw.”

## 25–45 seconds — Recommendation

“THE HOOK recommends leaving Sheehan in. Under the same assumptions, it estimates 47.6% win probability versus 44.0% for Kershaw—a +3.6-percentage-point advantage. Sheehan brings the stronger pooled strikeout profile and projected out rate against these hitters.”

## 45–65 seconds — Interaction

Select Alex Vesia in **Try another call**.

“I can test any plausible reliever. Every choice updates estimated win probability, expected runs, workload, and the explanation from one synchronized result.”

## 65–80 seconds — Credibility

Open **How It Works**.

“The estimates use 324,435 Statcast plate appearances, prior-only pooled profiles, a regularized outcome model, and 2,000 short-horizon simulations. Validation is chronological, and the model modestly beats a league-rate baseline.”

## 80–90 seconds — Close

“THE HOOK turns a high-pressure bullpen call into a comparison a manager, analyst, broadcaster, or fan can understand immediately—without pretending the counterfactual is certain.”
